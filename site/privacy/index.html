Privacy

Sandbox Capacity Probe is local first. The CLI sends no telemetry and does not send probe results, runtime metadata, container names, or host details to Sociobot.

Website storage

The free planner does not require an account. If you restore or purchase Planner Pro, the site stores the license token and its last verification result in your browser’s local storage. Saved scenarios also remain in local storage on that device. You can remove all of it by clearing site data.

License verification

The site sends the license token to the Sociobot billing API at most once per day when verification is due. Sociobot/Dodo acts as merchant of record for purchases and processes the contact and payment information required for the transaction. This site never receives card details. A browser allows one manual verification attempt per minute and honors a billing-service Retry-After response.

Offline use and logs

A service worker caches the public site shell for offline reading. Ordinary infrastructure logs may temporarily contain IP address, request path, browser user agent, and timestamp for security and reliability. There are no advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, or third-party scripts.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact privacy@sociobot.in. Include “Sandbox Capacity Probe” in the subject.
