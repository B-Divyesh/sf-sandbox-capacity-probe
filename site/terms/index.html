Terms

Sandbox Capacity Probe provides planning evidence, not a guarantee of production performance.

Results depend on your runtime, host, image cache, kernel, firewall, storage, and concurrent load.

Safe use

You are responsible for choosing and authorizing the runtime target. Do not probe infrastructure you do not control.

Use a non-production host when possible. Review the planned workload before starting test containers.

The CLI attempts cleanup after a run. An interrupted runtime may require the cleanup command in the README.

Availability

The planner, CSV export, CLI, safety controls, and JSON reports are free and need no account.
